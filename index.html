🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

Most problems have a simple fix. Work through the suggestions below if something does not behave as expected.

  • The page will not load. Check your internet connection, then refresh the page. If you are on a work or school network, it may block certain sites; try a different connection.
  • The download does not start. Allow pop-ups for the page and try again, or use a different browser. Edge and Chrome both handle the download page well.
  • Windows warns you about the file. This is a standard safety prompt for files downloaded from the internet. Choose the option to keep and open the file.
  • A pasted link is not recognised. Make sure you copied the full link, including the beginning and the end. Partial links usually fail.
  • A video fails to save. The source may not allow downloads, or your connection may have dropped. Try again, and check that your iPhone has free storage.
  • The video is not in your files. Look in the location where downloads are normally stored on your device; files are sometimes saved in a default folder rather than the one you expected.

If a problem persists, open the project page and leave a short description of what happened. Include what you were doing and what you saw on screen. That is usually enough for the maintainers to point you in the right direction.
